## Producing the export (AstroCast 1.6.0)

For the asteroids and Lilith, first tick **Points** on the Chart panel, in the Houses section ("Include asteroids and Lilith"). The box is off at every launch; ticking it recasts the chart on screen without saving it, and Clear Form and Load keep it. Then, on the Synastry & Composite panel, press **Calculate Composite** and **Export Composite MD**: Person B's chart is cast with the box's value at that moment, and a composite calculated before the tick carries no asteroids until it is calculated again. The composite then carries the composite five in its `## Asteroids and Lilith` section, directly after its Planetary Positions; neither person's own five is printed. A horary chart never carries them. With the box unticked, the default, the export is as before.

## Submission

Specify the subject, report period where relevant, context, focus, and any output override. For personal research use `Context: unvarnished and honest`. This does not permit external biography or activate medical analysis. An explicit voice instruction overrides the preset. For a blind test, remove names and biography while retaining chart settings and birth-time reliability.

## Family scope

Subject: the relationship as an entity. Research is candid third-person about the relationship; an address override does not turn composite placements into individual natal placements. Mathematical symmetry does not imply equal experience, power, or responsibility.

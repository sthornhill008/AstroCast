## Producing the export (AstroCast 1.6.0)

For the asteroids and Lilith, first tick **Points** on the Chart panel, in the Houses section ("Include asteroids and Lilith"). The box is off at every launch; ticking it recasts the chart on screen without saving it, and Clear Form and Load keep it. Then, on the Relocation panel, press **Calculate Relocation** and **Export Relocation MD**: a relocation calculated before the tick carries no asteroids until it is calculated again. The relocated chart then carries its own `## Asteroids and Lilith` section, directly after its Planetary Positions, with the natal positions in the relocated houses; the House Changes table has no rows for the five. A horary chart never carries them. With the box unticked, the default, the export is as before.

## Submission

Specify the subject, report period where relevant, context, focus, and any output override. For personal research use `Context: unvarnished and honest`. This does not permit external biography or activate medical analysis. An explicit voice instruction overrides the preset. For a blind test, remove names and biography while retaining chart settings and birth-time reliability.

## Family scope

Subject: the native at the supplied location. Distinguish unchanged natal longitudes and aspects from relocated angles/houses. Compare places only when their chart data is supplied; location changes symbolic emphasis, not guaranteed circumstances.

## Producing the export (AstroCast 1.6.0)

For the asteroids and Lilith, first tick **Points** on the Chart panel, in the Houses section ("Include asteroids and Lilith"). The box is off at every launch; ticking it recasts the chart on screen without saving it, and Clear Form and Load keep it. Then, on the Synastry & Composite panel, press **Calculate Synastry** and **Export Synastry MD**: Person B's chart is cast with the box's value at that moment, and a synastry calculated before the tick carries no asteroids until it is calculated again. Each person's chart then carries its own `## Asteroids and Lilith` section, and their contacts follow the Inter-Chart Aspects in `## Inter-Chart Aspects: Asteroids and Lilith`. A horary chart never carries them. With the box unticked, the default, the export is as before.

## Submission

Specify the subject, report period where relevant, context, focus, and any output override. For personal research use `Context: unvarnished and honest`. This does not permit external biography or activate medical analysis. An explicit voice instruction overrides the preset. For a blind test, remove names and biography while retaining chart settings and birth-time reliability.

## Family scope

Subject: both named people, with balanced attention and directional attribution. Research uses third-person for both. Explicit address-to-one/both overrides affect voice without changing planet ownership or house-overlay direction.

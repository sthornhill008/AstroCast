## Producing the export (AstroCast 1.6.0)

On the Chart panel, in the Houses section, tick **Points**, "Include asteroids and Lilith". The box is off at every launch; ticking it recasts the chart on screen without saving it, and Clear Form and Load keep it. Then export the chart's Markdown (**Save Markdown** under the Chart panel's Actions, or **Export Markdown** in the Export & Backup panel's Chart Export group). The export then carries a `## Asteroids and Lilith` section directly after the Out-of-Bounds Planets table: Ceres, Pallas, Juno, Vesta and Black Moon Lilith (the mean lunar apogee), with their aspects within 2°00′. A horary chart never carries them. The 1A (Blended) approach reads them as minor points; 1B (Ebertin) and 1C (Traditional) set the section aside. With the box unticked, the default, the export is as before and the reading says nothing about them.

## Submission

Specify the subject, report period where relevant, context, focus, and any output override. For personal research use `Context: unvarnished and honest`. This does not permit external biography or activate medical analysis. An explicit voice instruction overrides the preset. For a blind test, remove names and biography while retaining chart settings and birth-time reliability.

## Family scope

Subject: the native. Select Blended (default), Ebertin, or Traditional; the shared rules apply to all three. Supply this complete prompt, not an isolated preamble.

## Review

Check technical consistency, subject/voice, synthesis, relevant context, uncertainty, and output separately. Mark unavailable methods N/A. Record prompt version, model when available, input identity, date, resolved settings, and prose word count. Subjective resonance is not factual or scientific validation. Compare defaults, research, explicit voice override, missing data, and format override using the same inputs. Inspect every PDF page for clipping, wrapping, glyphs, stranded headings, and attribution.

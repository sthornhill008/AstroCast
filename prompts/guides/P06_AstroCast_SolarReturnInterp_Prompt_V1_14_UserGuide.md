## Producing the export (AstroCast 1.6.0)

For the asteroids and Lilith, first tick **Points** on the Chart panel, in the Houses section ("Include asteroids and Lilith"). The box is off at every launch; ticking it recasts the chart on screen without saving it, and Clear Form and Load keep it. Then, on the Returns panel, press **Calculate Solar Return** and **Export Solar Return MD**: a return calculated before the tick carries no asteroids until it is calculated again. The return chart then carries its own `## Asteroids and Lilith` section, directly after its Planetary Positions. A horary chart never carries them. With the box unticked, the default, the export is as before.

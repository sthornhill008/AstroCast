## Producing the export (AstroCast 1.7.0)

On the Chart panel, Calculate & Save (or Load) the first chart, Chart A: its house system is the Davison chart's. For the asteroids and Lilith, tick **Points** in the Houses section ("Include asteroids and Lilith"); the box is off at every launch, ticking it recasts the chart on screen without saving it, and Clear Form and Load keep it. Then, on the Synastry & Composite panel, select Person B in the Synastry section and, in the Davison Chart section below the Composite one, type any **Context** (optional; it prints as the header's Interpretation Context line), press **Calculate Davison**, then **Export Davison MD** (also in the Export panel's Synastry, Composite & Davison group); the file is `{A}_{B}_Davison.md`. Person B is cast with the Points box's value at that moment, and a Davison chart calculated before the tick carries no asteroids until it is calculated again. Any two saved charts will do, a person and an event included; a horary chart never carries the five. The export prints the chart complete under `## Davison Chart`, then `## Source Chart A` and `## Source Chart B`. The Davison chart is shown and exported only, not saved as a chart. With the box unticked, the default, the export has no asteroids section.

The header's **Midpoint Moment** is in Universal Time and the chart's clock reads `UTC+0 (explicit)`: no time zone applies to a point that may lie at sea. The **Midpoint Place** is the mean latitude and the mean longitude by the shorter arc, a mathematical point. Neither birth's time source is printed: say in your request when either birth time is unconfirmed, and the reading softens the houses, angles, Lots and parans accordingly.

## Submission

Specify the subject, report period where relevant, context, focus, and any output override. For personal research use `Context: unvarnished and honest`. This does not permit external biography or activate medical analysis. An explicit voice instruction overrides the preset. For a blind test, remove names and biography while retaining chart settings and birth-time reliability.

## Family scope

Subject: the relationship as an entity. Research is candid third-person about the relationship; an address override does not turn Davison placements into individual natal placements. The Davison chart is a real chart whose planets, angles and houses are the relationship's own, not either person's. Symmetry in time and place does not imply equal experience, power, or responsibility.

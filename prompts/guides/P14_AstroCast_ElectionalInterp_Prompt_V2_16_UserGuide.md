## Submission

For a blind test, remove names and biography while retaining chart settings and birth-time reliability.

## Family scope

Subject: the querent, proposed act, and candidate tradeoffs. Apply real-world constraints before astrological ranking; a valid result may be no feasible candidate. The elected act must match the question. For a medical election (a surgery, a treatment, an appointment) the reading compares the candidates as the historical method reads them and recommends no date: scheduling follows clinical needs. Notes and added context are treated as your account of the situation, not as verified fact.

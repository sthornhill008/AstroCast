## Resolution

Resolve report mode and subject first. Then apply explicit local settings, the research preset, compatible inherited settings, and family defaults. The latest conflicting explicit setting wins. Quoted, negated, or incidental occurrences in chart data do not activate it. Explicit voice wins. **Unvarnished mode** and its established aliases remain tone-only. Neither control imports biography, activates medical analysis, or changes format.

## Communication

Use the correct family subject throughout: native, both synastry people, relationship entity, querent/question, event, institution, nation, or cycle. Check identities, chart roles, zodiac, houses, coordinates, precision, time reliability, units, and contradictions. Resolve orb notation per export/column from schema and available longitudes. Medical analysis requires an affirmative request. Identifiable public-figure medical restrictions remain family rules and are not bypassed by research or event-chart status.

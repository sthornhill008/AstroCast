# AstroCast Current Prompt Manifest

**Release date:** 2026-10-22  
**Shared contract:** 1.0  
**Current prompts:** 30 (28 interpretive/reference; 2 data/graphic)
**Engine:** this release matches AstroCast 1.7.0 as landed (master `9e227eb`)  

This release adds P30, the Davison chart prompt, for the Davison Chart Export new in AstroCast 1.7.0 (branches Davison and DavisonPanel): a new family in `Davison\`, recast from P13 v1.12 for a real chart cast at the midpoint of the two births in time and place, with its own History and User Guide. Three prompts take rulings from the 1.5.0 library work's leftovers (Steve, 2026-10-22): P03 v3.12 reads a Birth Time Source of Not specified as Unknown everywhere; P28 v1.4 counts a primary direction toward the Sensitive-Period Framing protocol's fourth trigger as it counts a solar arc or a progression; P11 v1.10 labels the title page's subject line by the chart's type (Born, Event, Asked). The other 26 prompts are unchanged. Earlier prompt versions, their Histories and User Guides, and the earlier manifests and usage maps are archived in `Prompts.zip` (kept by the maintainer, outside this repository) under `Prompts\`, where each keeps its library path. The 1.6.0 work stands as the 2026-10-20d manifest described it: 13 readers read the asteroids and Lilith (Ceres, Pallas, Juno, Vesta and Black Moon Lilith, the mean lunar apogee), and P30 joins them as the fourteenth; P16 and P17 set them aside, and the other 14 read reports that never carry them.

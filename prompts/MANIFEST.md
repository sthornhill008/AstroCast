**Release date:** 2026-11-28

This release gives fifteen prompts a new version, for the exports of AstroCast 1.7.2 and after a review of the whole library (2026-11-27; Steve's rulings of 2026-11-27 and 2026-11-28): P03 v3.13, P04 v2.13, P05 v2.5, P06 v1.15, P07 v1.14, P10 v1.6, P11 v1.11, P12 v1.14, P14 v2.16, P15 v2.11, P17 v1.10, P24 v1.7, P27 v1.7, P28 v1.5 and P29 v1.1. For AstroCast 1.7.2: P03, P28 and P29 read the marks, the footnotes and the Natures of the Promissor Stars table of the Primary Directions table; P06 reads the solar return's three time-lord sections for the one year the return opens; P10 and P14 read the names 1.7.2 prints ("Enters Aries (retrograde)", "Real Estate Listing", "Waning Crescent"); P05 reads a daily export that prints no house for an unknown birth time; P03 sizes its reading to a report that may be a single day. From the review: one way to submit P12, P14 and P15 (the whole prompt, then one of its templates, then the export); medical content on an operator's request only in P03, P10 and P11, and no recommended date for a medical election in P14 or from a timeline in P24; nothing computed outside the export in P04 and P15; one timing window in P15; the solar return's voice, data note and dated windows in P06; the Sequence number in P07; P17 complete in itself, with its orb table and its routes; and in P27 a single image on request and a rule on what may be delivered. P29 v1.1 adds a figure and a competing-matches table to its rectification notes, a trial-shift check on request, researched events for a public figure and a "Rectification only" report. The guides of P23 and P24 name the exports those prompts read. Each prompt's History lists its changes. The other 15 prompts are unchanged. The 2026-10-22 release added P30, the Davison chart prompt, for the Davison Chart Export new in AstroCast 1.7.0. Earlier prompt versions, their Histories and User Guides, and the earlier manifests and usage maps are archived in `Prompts.zip` (kept by the maintainer, outside this repository) under `Prompts\`, where each keeps its library path. The 1.6.0 work stands as the 2026-10-20d manifest described it: 13 readers read the asteroids and Lilith (Ceres, Pallas, Juno, Vesta and Black Moon Lilith, the mean lunar apogee), and P30 joins them as the fourteenth; P16 and P17 set them aside, and the other 14 read reports that never carry them.

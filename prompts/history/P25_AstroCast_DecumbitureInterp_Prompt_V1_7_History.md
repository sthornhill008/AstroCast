## Evaluation Rubric (13 dimensions, 1–5 scale)

| Dimension | 1 (Poor) | 3 (Adequate) | 5 (Excellent) |
|-----------|----------|--------------|---------------|
| **Significator Completeness** | Major significators missed | Most assessed, some thin | All 7 significators assessed with appropriate depth; significator collapses (L1=L6, L6=L7, L8=L10) named explicitly when present |
| **Illness Identification** | 6th house not assessed | 6th cusp sign noted but not developed | 6th cusp zodiacal anatomy fully applied; L6 condition shapes illness assessment; anaretic-cusp transitions read structurally if present |
| **Moon Prominence** | Moon buried | Moon assessed but not central | Moon is the primary dynamic indicator; phase, speed, and applying aspects drive the prognosis |
| **Critical Days** | Not calculated | Dates listed without analysis | All 4 critical days calculated with transit conditions and severity assessment when transit data is available; absence of transit data reported explicitly without fabrication |
| **Danger Assessment** | Skipped or alarmist | Some indicators noted | Favorable and unfavorable indicators systematically tallied; proportional assessment |
| **Medical Disclaimer** | Missing or minimal | Present once | Prominent on title page AND at end; properly frames the entire interpretation |
| **Classical Framework Fidelity** | Modern astrology applied instead | Mixed classical/modern | Consistent classical method (Culpeper/Saunders/Cornell); traditional rulerships; decumbiture-specific reasoning |
| **Context Integration** | Context ignored | Context mentioned once | Illness context woven throughout; chart testimony cross-referenced with stated condition |
| **Narrative Coherence** | Disconnected observations | Some flow | All layers build toward a unified prognosis |
| **Accessible Language** | Heavy jargon | Mixed | Consistently clear; classical terms explained in context |
| **Honest Proportionality** | Only dire or only positive | Both mentioned superficially | Favorable and unfavorable indicators balanced honestly; severity proportional to chart testimony |
| **Sect Integration** | Sect ignored | Sect mentioned | Out-of-sect malefic identified as primary aggravator; in-sect benefic as primary healer; house placements interpreted; sect-as-significator-modifier surfaced when sect bodies coincide with role-significators |
| **House System Fidelity** | House system not identified or methodologically inappropriate (e.g., Whole-Sign for a chart where intermediate cusps materially shape the L6/L8 reading) | House system identified; default applied without consideration | House system identified explicitly; system selection appropriate to decumbiture tradition (Regiomontanus preferred on historical-fidelity grounds, with Placidus or other systems used as deliberate operator choice); cusp degrees and ruler assignments traced to the system in use |

### Scoring
- **52–65:** Publication quality
- **39–51:** Good draft
- **26–38:** Adequate but thin
- **Below 26:** Needs rework

- **No functional astrological methodology changes.**

### v1.2 (2026-05-05)
- **Action Item 1 — Critical-day transit data handling:** Layer 6 and Output Structure Section 6 updated with explicit handling for absent forward-ephemeris data. AI states absence as a methodology limitation, calculates dates from lunar geometry alone, and does not invent transit positions. Engine-side counterpart (AstroCast adding a Critical-Day Transits section to Event-chart exports when EventType=Event and Notes contain "decumbiture") remains a separate engine-side TODO.
- **Action Item 2 — Test-mode output override:** Added `"Test mode — chat output, then PDF if requested"` to Override Protocol. Distinguishes audit-campaign runs from production runs without weakening the production PDF default. Flagged as candidate for library-wide promotion across all AstroCast interpretation prompts.
- **Action Item 3 — Significator Collapse subsection:** Added to Layer 1 (Patient). Names common collapses (L1=L6, L6=L7, L8=L10, L1=L7) and gives the classical reading for each. Worked example: L1=L6 from the empirically-validated sore-throat case under Regiomontanus. Compliance gate updated to verify collapses are named explicitly when present.
- **Action Item 4 — Ascendant degree convention:** Refined from two-tier (early ≤5°, late ≥25°) to four-tier (nascent 0°–5°, early-established 6°–14°, mid-established/advanced 15°–24°, late/chronic 25°–30°). Same convention applied to MC degree where used.
- **Action Item 5 — House System recommendation (headline):** Added "House System and Methodology" subsection to front-matter explaining the historical-fidelity argument for Regiomontanus in decumbiture (Lilly's system; Culpeper was Lilly-adjacent; Placidus's adoption postdates the founding decumbiture authorities). Front-matter table includes new HouseSystem row with Regiomontanus override syntax. Filename convention extended to suffix the date with a system identifier when non-default systems are used (e.g., `20260428R` for Regiomontanus). Default house system unchanged (Placidus retained for AstroCast cross-library consistency); recommendation will be revisited when accumulated cases support a default change. Empirical worked case (2026-04-28 sore-throat decumbiture) supports the recommendation: under Regiomontanus, the 6th cusp shifted from Taurus (Placidus) to anaretic Aries, moving L6 from peregrine Venus to Mars-in-domicile and surfacing an interpretively meaningful L1=L6 collapse not present under Placidus, with the Mars-as-L6 reading materially better matching the empirical course on illness-character and severity dimensions.
- **Sect-as-significator-modifier:** Worked example added to Layer 1 sect discussion. When a sect-relevant body (in-sect benefic, out-of-sect malefic, in-sect malefic, out-of-sect benefic) is also a role-significator (L1, L6, L7, L8, L10), the sect assignment becomes interpretively load-bearing and must be named explicitly.
- **Anaretic degree:** Added to glossary topic group "Classical Severity Indicators." Convention for anaretic house cusps codified ("the matters of that house are about to transition into the energies of the next sign").
- **Evaluation Rubric — Dimension 13 (House System Fidelity):** Added to surface house-system selection as a deliberate audit dimension. Scoring brackets adjusted from /60 to /65 (publication-quality threshold raised proportionally from 48 to 52).
- **Length target framing:** Updated from "standard" to "guideline; clarity over brevity" in front-matter. Compliance gate updated correspondingly. The 2,000-3,500 word range remains a reference, but expansion is explicitly permitted where the chart genuinely warrants it.
- **Companion document reference:** Header updated to reference the companion audit document (`AstroCast_Prompt_Audit_Opus47_Continuity.md`), which holds empirical validation logs and the worked-case study for the v1.2 methodology decisions.
- **No functional astrological methodology changes.** All v1.2 changes are spec refinements, gap closures, methodology framing improvements, and an audit-derived recommendation. The classical decumbiture framework (Culpeper / Saunders / Cornell) is unchanged.

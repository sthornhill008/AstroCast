Glossary of Terms

Considerations before judgment

Radicality and planetary hour. Radicality is a traditional fitness check. The Sun governs the planetary hour at the chart time and rules Mars's sign, an indirect link.

Late Ascendant. A rising degree beyond 27°. Here 28°18' Scorpio cautions that the situation is far along.

Void of course and Via Combusta. A void Moon makes no major applying aspect before leaving its sign. Via Combusta is a traditional caution zone across late Libra and early Scorpio. Both cautions are absent here.

Significators and planet condition

L1, Lx and co-significator. L1 is Mars for you, Lx is Venus for your friend, and the Moon also represents you and tracks events.

Essential dignity. Domicile is rulership of a sign, here Venus in Libra. Face is a minor dignity, here Mars in Leo. Peregrine means without essential dignity, as for the Moon.

Detriment and fall. Detriment is the sign opposite a planet's domicile, such as Libra for Mars. Fall is the sign opposite its exaltation, as Aries is for Saturn.

Accidental condition and house of joy. House position, motion and contact affect a planet's ability to act. The 9th is cadent, or less able to act. The 11th is succedent, or sustaining, and is Venus's traditional house of joy.

Cazimi, combustion and under the beams. These are degrees of solar proximity. Cazimi is within 0°17' of the Sun, combustion extends out to 8°, and the beams extend to 17°. Both main significators are clear of these conditions.

Besiegement and station direct. Besiegement is enclosure between the traditional malefics, Mars and Saturn. A station direct is a turn to forward motion, as Venus made about a day and a half before the chart.

Sect and hayz. Sect aligns certain planets with day or night. Hayz adds hemisphere and sign conditions to that alignment, as with Jupiter here.

Reception and perfection

Dispositor and reception. A dispositor rules the sign occupied by another planet. Reception compares placements with a planet's dignities. It is mutual when both sides have a corresponding dignity, as the Sun and Mars do by domicile.

Perfection, applying and separating. Perfection is a completed link that brings the matter together. An applying aspect approaches exactness. A separating one, such as the Venus-Mars sextile, is moving away.

Translation and collection of light. Translation carries contact from one planet to another through an intermediary. In collection both parties apply to the same slower planet.

Antiscia and declination parallel. Antiscia are solstice-axis mirror points. A declination parallel is closeness north or south of the celestial equator on the same side. Neither links Mars and Venus here.

The Moon, lots and stars

Moon mansion and out of bounds. A mansion is one of 28 traditional lunar divisions, here the benefic 24th. Out of bounds means beyond the Sun's normal declination range, as for Pluto.

Arabic Part or Hermetic Lot. A derived zodiacal point. Fortune describes practical circumstances here, while Victory adds testimony about allies.

Behenian fixed star. A star given special weight in the traditional catalogue used by the export. Altair is the Behenian star conjunct the Moon here.

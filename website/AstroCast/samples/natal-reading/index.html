Glossary of Terms

Planetary strength and condition

  • Domicile. A planet’s home sign. Venus in Libra holds the strongest sign-based dignity credited in this chart.
  • Exaltation, fall, and detriment. Exaltation is a sign of special standing, and its opposite is fall. Detriment is the sign opposite a planet’s home sign. Libra is Saturn’s exaltation and the Sun’s fall. Leo is Saturn’s detriment.
  • Peregrine. A planet with no essential dignity under the scoring convention used. The export gives the Moon, Mercury, and Mars peregrine status.
  • Sect and hayz. Sect is the traditional division into daytime and nighttime teams. Hayz adds favorable conditions of hemisphere and sign gender. The Sun and Saturn qualify in the export.
  • In-sect benefic and out-of-sect malefic. Jupiter is the traditionally supportive planet aligned with this day chart. Mars is the traditionally challenging planet working against its sect and also rules your Ascendant.
  • Under the beams. Moderately obscured by proximity to the Sun, conventionally within about 8°–17°. Venus has this condition alongside domicile dignity.
  • Besiegement. Placement between Mars and Saturn without benefic intervention under the export’s convention. The chart lists this condition for Pluto in Leo.
  • Angular and cadent houses. Houses 1, 4, 7, and 10 are angular. Houses 3, 6, 9, and 12 are cadent, often linked with preparation, learning, or service. The export uses a separate five-degree distance test when listing bodies near the exact angles.
  • Stationing retrograde. Slowing to an apparent standstill and then moving backward in zodiacal longitude. Uranus had recently turned retrograde in the supplied chart.
  • Triplicity rulers. Planets with traditional rulership over an element. The water triplicity sequence of Venus, Mars, and the Moon supplies the early, middle, and later life-phase symbolism here.

Planetary relationships and the Moon

  • Dispositorship and final dispositor. A planet answers to the ruler of its sign in the traditional rulership scheme. Your planetary chains end with Venus in its own sign of Libra.
  • Mutual reception. Two planets each occupy a sign where the other has dignity. The Sun–Saturn exchange here uses exaltation on one side and domicile on the other.
  • Conjunction, sextile, square, trine, and opposition. The major configurations used here, centered on 0°, 60°, 90°, 120°, and 180° respectively. An orb measures the distance from the exact angle.
  • Semisquare and quincunx. A 45-degree aspect of friction and a 150-degree aspect of adjustment, respectively. The Moon–Mars semisquare and Jupiter–Uranus quincunx are both close.
  • Applying and separating. An applying aspect is moving toward exactness. A separating aspect has passed exactness. The Moon is applying to its sextile with Saturn, while Mercury’s square to Mars is separating.
  • Waning gibbous. The phase after the full Moon and before the last quarter. Your Moon is in the middle of this phase, traditionally associated in this reading with digesting and sharing experience.
  • Lunar mansion and void of course. Mansions are traditional divisions of the Moon’s path. Your Moon occupies the seventh, Al-Dhira, and is active, with a major aspect still to perfect in Gemini.

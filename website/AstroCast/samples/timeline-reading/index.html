Glossary of Terms

Events and precision

  • Exact pass and residual orb. A pass is a moment a contact perfects. The residual orb is its printed distance from exactness. An exact row at 0°00′ is shown at arcminute resolution.
  • Entry, exit and transit window. Entry and exit are the first and last crossings of the allowed orb for a passage. The transit can leave and re-enter orb between listed milestones.
  • Station and ingress. A station is the apparent change between forward and backward motion. An ingress is a change of zodiac sign.
  • TR and NA. TR marks a transiting body and NA a natal target. A TR-to-TR aspect is a general sky contact. ASC is the Ascendant, MC the Midheaven and IC the lower meridian opposite the Midheaven.
  • Aspect abbreviations. cnj = conjunction, sqr = square, opp = opposition, tri = trine, sxt = sextile, par = parallel, cpar = contraparallel. A square or opposition stresses a difference, while a trine or sextile describes an avenue of cooperation.
  • Midpoint exact. A recorded contact to the halfway point between two natal factors, as measured on AstroCast’s midpoint dial. A priority midpoint includes the Sun, Moon, Ascendant or Midheaven.
  • Event Type B. A chart cast at a timed transit event using the base location and compared with the natal chart. The cluster recommendations point to specific rows with a printed UTC minute.

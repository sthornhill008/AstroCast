AstroCast website - font provenance
Recorded 2026-11-15, on Maple.

The five font files in this folder are byte-identical copies of the files
in the PhotoDB website's fonts folder, whose own PROVENANCE.txt (recorded
2026-09-06) is the source of the release details below. Nothing was fetched
again for AstroCast; each copy was checked against the SHA-256 given here.

Both faces are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The licence
permits use, embedding and redistribution of unmodified copies provided
each copy carries the copyright notice and the licence, which is what the
two LICENSE-*.txt files beside the fonts are for. Neither face is sold on
its own and neither is modified.

## 10. Glossary of terms

### The annual chart

Solar return. The chart for the Sun’s return to its natal longitude, interpreted for one annual cycle.

Ascendant and Midheaven. The rising degree and the meridian degree. They describe annual orientation and public direction.

House and angularity. A house describes a life area. Angular houses are the first, fourth, seventh and tenth. Closeness to an exact angle is a separate measure.

Chart ruler and dispositor. The chart ruler governs the rising sign. A dispositor governs the sign occupied by another planet; chains can loop or have several endpoints.

### Condition and planetary relationships

Dignity, peregrine and fall. Dignity describes a planet’s standing in its sign under the export’s scheme. Peregrine means lacking a scored major dignity. Fall describes a challenging sign condition.

Sect and hayz. Sect distinguishes day and night conditions. Hayz is an additional traditional condition of functional authority.

Mutual reception. Two planets occupy signs where each has a dignity. Here the Sun and Saturn each occupy the other’s exaltation sign.

Retrograde and station. Apparent backward motion and the turning point of that motion. A station concentrates a planet’s symbolism.

Applying and separating. An aspect is moving toward or away from exactness under the export’s convention. Neither supplies an event date by itself.

T-square and quincunx. A T-square combines an opposition and two squares. A quincunx is a 150-degree relationship read here as adjustment.

### The Moon and supporting techniques

Void of course and lunar mansion. The void-of-course label describes the Moon’s traditional aspect condition. A mansion is one of 28 lunar divisions with inherited symbolic meanings.

Declination and out of bounds. Declination measures north-south position from the celestial equator. Out of bounds means beyond the usual solar declination limit.

Parallel and contraparallel. Bodies at similar declinations or equal north/south distances. These supply a separate layer from zodiacal longitude.

Midpoint and antiscion. A midpoint combines two positions mathematically. Antiscia are mirrored positions; a contra-antiscion is the corresponding opposition mirror.

Fortune and Spirit. Calculated Lots concerning circumstances and intention. “Part” and “Lot” name the same point in this reading.

### Annual timing

Profection and lord of the year. A yearly house activation and the planet ruling its sign. The return export supplies the birthday year opening.

Firdaria. A sequence of planetary major periods and sub-periods used here as background timing.

Zodiacal Releasing. A timing sequence from a Lot. Level 1 is a long phase and Level 2 a shorter phase. The export classifies the active Leo phases as succedent.
